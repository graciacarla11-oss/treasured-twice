The shipping promise

Free shipping at $50

Qualifying orders with a merchandise subtotal of $50 or more receive free shipping. For orders below $50, the customer pays the shipping charge. An order totaling exactly $50 in merchandise qualifies. Tax and shipping charges do not count toward the $50 threshold.

Merchandise subtotal of $50 or more
Free shipping — $0.00
Merchandise subtotal below $50
Customer-paid shipping, disclosed before payment

Shipping availability depends on the item and destination and is confirmed before purchase. Free shipping does not make a pickup-only item eligible for shipment.

Your order summary will show merchandise, any discounts, shipping, applicable tax, and the final total separately before you pay. A shipping charge that has not been confirmed is not a $0 charge.

Processing & delivery

The processing timeframe, available carrier service, and any delivery estimate are confirmed for your order before payment. We do not advertise a universal shipping rate or guaranteed arrival date.

Processing time covers preparing and packing your order. Transit time begins after the carrier receives the package. These are separate stages; a delivery estimate is not a promise of same-day dispatch.

Carrier estimates can change because of the destination, weather, holidays, or service disruptions. If we cannot ship within the timeframe agreed for your order, we will contact you with an update and the option to cancel for a prompt full refund. We obtain consent to shipping delays when required and cancel and refund when required consent is not received.

Your delivery information

For a shipped order, provide the following through the secure ordering process supplied for your purchase:

  • The recipient’s full name.
  • The complete delivery street address, including an apartment, suite, or unit number when applicable.
  • City, state or region, ZIP or postal code, and country.
  • An email address for order communication. A phone number may be requested if the delivery service requires it.

Check your shipping address before confirming payment; a billing address may be different. Contact us promptly about corrections. Changes after dispatch may not be possible. If an address error affects delivery, we will review the available options with you and disclose any additional agreed shipping cost before charging it.

Tracking & delivery support

When tracking is available for your shipment, it shows the carrier’s latest updates. A label being created does not mean the carrier has received the package.

To request tracking or an order update, or to report a missing, damaged, or incorrect delivery, email hello@shoptreasuredtwice.com with your order reference or item SKU. For damage, keep the packaging and include photos if available.

We will help investigate shipment problems and arrange the remedy required for your order. Carrier delays do not remove your applicable consumer rights.

Placing an order

Item requests on this website currently begin by email. We confirm availability and the next steps before payment. Sending a request does not complete a purchase.

Shipping details and the full order total must be confirmed before payment. Please do not send payment card numbers, passwords, or other sensitive information by email.
